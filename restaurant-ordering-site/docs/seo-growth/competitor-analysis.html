Schema observations and technical limits

Raw HTML contains Organization/WebSite schemas on the reviewed sites; Blink also exposes breadcrumbs and page/image entities. Bhookly and Foodnerd use SoftwareApplication; ReeOrder includes application, offer, rating and FAQ entities. Their presence does not verify underlying claims. ReeOrder’s fetched canonical is relative; the review records this without declaring it a ranking defect. No technical pattern was copied merely because a competitor uses it.

Public mentions: verified examples, not backlink totals

  • Collyer Law’s own Blink investment-advice announcement is a primary business mention.
  • Startup Syndicate’s restaurant-technology event account names Indolj among participants. This supports industry collaboration as a prospect category; it is not an invitation or current event commitment.
  • Profit’s Blink coverage is an example of relevant business reporting. Editorial relationships and link attributes must be evaluated separately.

Highest-priority opportunity

Restrova’s technical foundation is already functioning on the live site. The next defensible advantage is clearer product evaluation: specific workflows, accurate costs, genuine screens, approved local implementation stories and a measurement chain from organic visit to paying restaurant. Content quantity, copied superlatives and unsupported integration claims do not establish that advantage.

See asset requirements, content calendar, outreach kit and measurement plan.
