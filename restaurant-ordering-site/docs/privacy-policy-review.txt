SOURCE-VERIFIED WEBSITE BEHAVIOR
- src/app/layout.tsx loads Meta Pixel globally after interaction readiness and tracks PageView. It also includes a no-JavaScript tracking image. This is not a consent gate.
- src/components/RestrovaScheduleDemo.tsx sends a Meta Lead event after successful storage of a qualified demo request.
- src/components/ContactForm.tsx collects campaign parameters, ad identifiers, and fbclid from the page URL for enquiry attribution.
- src/lib/restrova-demo-session.ts and src/app/api/demo/* use the restrova_demo_draft_v6 cookie during scheduling.
- src/lib/restrova-demo.ts applies automatic business qualification; results influence scheduling availability and lead categorization. The policy discloses this rather than claiming there is no automated processing.
- src/lib/restrova-sheets.ts forwards enquiry/scheduling data to Google Sheets through Google Apps Script.
- src/lib/site.ts contains the existing support@lancers.dev contact.
- No separate Google Analytics integration or cookie preference manager was found in the inspected application source. Google Sheets is described as an enquiry system, not website analytics.
