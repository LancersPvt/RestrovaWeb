RESTROVA — POS, INVENTORY AND DELIVERY SERVICE UPDATE
Reviewed 2 November 2026. Local repository implementation; not deployed.

SCOPE AND AUDIT
The existing six-service route is statically generated. The three requested pages already had distinct H1s, introductory product descriptions, four-step workflows, feature sections, buying checklists, demo questions, FAQs, pricing and related links. They used self-canonical production URLs, SoftwareApplication and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, plus the site's shared identity graph.
The missing detail was the operational handoff between ordering, stock decisions, preparation, dispatch and settlement. Inventory lacked a contextual delivery link, and delivery lacked a contextual inventory link. The new content fills those gaps without replacing existing sections.
Public service-page retrieval through the browsing tool was unavailable during this review; the audit and validation use the current repository and its local production build. User-reported ChatGPT Search results are context, not independently reproduced measurements.

IMPLEMENTATION
1. POS: concurrent pickup/app order example, availability check, kitchen handoff, dispatch, unavailable-item exception and three problem/evaluation/demo checks.
2. Inventory: shortage during service, stock-to-menu decision, accepted-order response, packing handoff, cancellation/stock correction exception and three evaluation checks.
3. Delivery: preparation-to-packing-to-rider workflow, ownership at each handoff, interrupted tracking/permissions scenario, and assignment/tracking/settlement evaluation checks.
4. All three: links among the requested service pages and to pricing, FAQ, apps, About and Contact; affordability framed through the existing sales-based fee and agreed operating scope.
5. POS only: one practical paragraph asking a Chakwal restaurant to bring its real branch address, delivery areas, connectivity and devices. No new city page or local-office/customer claim.
6. Server components and plain links/HTML. No client bundle, dependency, form, pricing logic, global style or route changes. Existing content objects are identical except the intended title and description fields.
